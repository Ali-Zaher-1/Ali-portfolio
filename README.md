## 🎯 Crown Swarm Transition

The signature feature of this portfolio is the **crown swarm animation** that triggers during section transitions:

- 60 small golden crowns burst out from multiple spawn points across the entire screen
- Each crown flies in a random direction with unique rotation
- Crowns range from 18-60px in size for variety
- The animation takes 2 seconds for a slow, cinematic feel
- A gentle gold flash accompanies the transition
- The main crown pulses and glows brighter during the effect

---

## 💧 Water Ripple Cursor Effect

As you move your mouse across the portfolio:

- Golden ripples expand outward from your cursor position
- Each ripple has multiple rings (outer gold, middle light gold, inner glow)
- Ripples fade away gradually like water settling
- Creates the sensation of walking through water

---

## 🖱️ Custom Cursor

- Golden dot that follows your mouse precisely
- Larger ring that trails behind with smooth delay
- Ring expands when hovering over clickable elements
- Perfect for the cinematic space theme

## 🎯 Navigation Guide

| Action | Result |
|--------|--------|
| Scroll normally inside any section | Read content |
| At the **bottom** of a section, scroll again | Move to the **next** section |
| At the **top** of a section, scroll again | Move to the **previous** section |
| Click navigation links in the navbar | Jump directly to any section |
| Click progress dots on the right | Jump directly to any section |
